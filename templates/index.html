Recycling Wind Turbine Blades using Bioresins

Towards a cleaner and sustainable energy future

About the Project

Wind turbine blades are traditionally non-recyclable, creating massive waste. Our project introduces bioresin technology, enabling blades to be recycled into valuable materials. This promotes sustainability, reduces landfill waste, and contributes to a greener energy ecosystem.

Materials Used

  • Glass Fiber Reinforced Plastics (GFRP)
  • Carbon Fiber Reinforced Plastics (CFRP)
  • Epoxy & Polyester Resins
  • Balsa Wood & PET Foam
  • Bioresins / Natural Fibers
